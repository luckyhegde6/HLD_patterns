# Interview Walkthrough — Google Maps

Clarify map coverage, routing modes, traffic freshness, ETA target and offline support. Estimate tile QPS, route QPS and probe events separately. Draw tile CDN, geocoding index, versioned road graph and traffic pipeline. Deep dive graph search, map matching, closures, reroute hysteresis and stale traffic fallback. Close with p99 route latency, ETA error, graph freshness, CDN hit ratio and privacy controls.
