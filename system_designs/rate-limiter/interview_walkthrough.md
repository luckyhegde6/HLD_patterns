# Interview Walkthrough

1. Clarify quota scope, burst and fairness.
2. Choose algorithm and explain state.
3. Walk atomic check/consume.
4. Discuss distributed consistency and key cardinality.
5. Cover outage policy, hot keys and client retry behavior.
6. Explain metrics, tuning and cost.

Follow-ups: multi-region? weighted requests? noisy tenant? fail-open vs fail-closed?
