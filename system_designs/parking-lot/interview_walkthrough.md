# Interview Walkthrough

1. Clarify vehicle/spot compatibility and entry/exit workflow.
2. Model inventory and atomic assignment.
3. Walk ticket and payment lifecycle.
4. Cover concurrency, offline gates and sensor mismatch.
5. Explain pricing, reconciliation and observability.

Follow-ups: reservations? EV charging? lost ticket? payment timeout?
