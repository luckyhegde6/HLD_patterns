# Interview Walkthrough

1. Clarify building/elevator topology and safety requirements.
2. Model calls, car state and constraints.
3. Explain dispatch scoring and fairness.
4. Walk request-to-assignment-to-stop sequence.
5. Cover sensor/network/controller failures.
6. Discuss telemetry, simulation and operational tuning.

Follow-ups: destination dispatch? zoning? peak traffic? maintenance mode? fire service?
