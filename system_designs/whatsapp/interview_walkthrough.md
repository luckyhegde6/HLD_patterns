# Interview Walkthrough — WhatsApp-Style Messaging

Clarify 1:1/group scale, ordering, offline retention, device count and E2E boundary. Estimate messages/sec and recipient fanout/sec. Define send, sync and receipt APIs. Draw durable append before acknowledgement and asynchronous fanout. Deep dive sequence allocation, dedupe, reconnect cursor, hot groups, offline queues and device-key changes. Close with privacy/metadata trade-offs and metrics: accepted latency, delivery lag, reconnect backlog, socket churn and duplicate rate.
