# Interview Walkthrough — Ride-Hailing

Clarify city scale, peak request rate, pickup SLA, driver GPS cadence, cancellation and payment requirements. Estimate location events/sec separately from ride requests. Draw geo candidate lookup, offer lease and atomic accept. Deep dive trip state transitions, stale GPS, payment reconciliation, surge pricing and safety. Close with dispatch latency, match rate, stale-location rate, cancellation and payment reconciliation metrics.
