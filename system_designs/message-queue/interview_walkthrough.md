# Interview Walkthrough

1. Clarify producer/consumer workflow and delivery guarantees.
2. Estimate throughput, retention and partition count.
3. Explain partitioning, ordering and consumer groups.
4. Walk publish, consume and offset commit.
5. Cover retries, DLQ, replay and idempotency.
6. Discuss replication, lag, scaling and cost.

Follow-ups: poison messages? hot keys? consumer rebalance? external side effects?
