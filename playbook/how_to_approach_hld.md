# How to Approach HLD Interviews

---

## Step-by-Step Strategy

1. Clarify requirements
2. Identify constraints
3. Estimate scale
4. Define components
5. Design data model
6. Address scaling
7. Discuss failures and trade-offs

---

## Control the Interview

- Speak in structured steps
- Pause to confirm assumptions
- Narrate trade-offs explicitly

---

## Senior Signal

Clarity > complexity.
Confidence comes from structure.
