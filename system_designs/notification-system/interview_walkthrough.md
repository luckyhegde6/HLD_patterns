# Interview Walkthrough

1. Clarify channels, consent and delivery semantics.
2. Estimate fanout, throughput and provider quotas.
3. Design event intake, preference check and queueing.
4. Walk send, retry and receipt processing.
5. Cover dedupe, provider failover and opt-out races.
6. Discuss localization, observability and cost.

Follow-ups: consent revocation after enqueue? provider outage? duplicate webhook?
