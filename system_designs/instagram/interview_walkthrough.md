# Interview Walkthrough — Instagram

Clarify media types, story lifetime, privacy, feed freshness and follower distribution. Estimate uploads, stored bytes, feed reads and CDN egress. Define upload/post/feed APIs and media publication invariant. Draw media processing, feed candidate pipeline and CDN path. Deep dive privacy changes, expiry, fanout skew, retries and deletion. Finish with metrics: processing age, feed p99, CDN hit ratio, fanout lag and privacy leakage tests.
