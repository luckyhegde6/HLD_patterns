# Interview Walkthrough

- Clarify payment lifecycle and compliance scope.
- Define idempotency and state transitions.
- Walk authorization, capture and webhook handling.
- Explain ledger invariants and reconciliation.
- Cover ambiguous timeouts, duplicates and provider outage.
- Discuss observability and operations.

Follow-ups: partial capture, duplicate webhook, timeout, refund race, settlement mismatch.
