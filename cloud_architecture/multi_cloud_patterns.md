# Multi-Cloud Patterns

Multi-cloud architectures use multiple cloud providers to reduce vendor lock-in
or improve resilience.

---

## Why Multi-Cloud Is Hard

- Operational complexity
- Networking challenges
- Data consistency issues

---

## Common Patterns

### 1. Active-Passive
- One primary cloud
- Failover to secondary

### 2. Active-Active
- Traffic split across providers
- Complex data synchronization

---

## When to Use Multi-Cloud

- Regulatory requirements
- Extreme availability needs

---

## Interview Insight

Most companies should avoid multi-cloud unless forced.
Complexity is the real cost.
