# Trade-offs

| Choice | Trade-off |
|---|---|
| Central allocator vs per-floor | Global fit vs local resilience |
| Reservation vs immediate occupancy | Prevent races vs temporary holds |
| Sensor-driven vs ledger-driven occupancy | Physical accuracy vs transactional consistency |
| Dynamic pricing vs fixed rates | Demand optimization vs simplicity/transparency |

Never rely on a cache alone to guarantee spot uniqueness.
