# Common System Design Interview Questions

---

## Architecture
- Why this database?
- Why caching here?
- Why not synchronous calls?

---

## Scaling
- What breaks first?
- How do you handle hot keys?
- How do you reshard?

---

## Reliability
- What happens on failure?
- How do you recover?
- What data can be lost?

---

## Consistency
- Why eventual consistency?
- Where is strong consistency required?

---

## Behavioral Overlay
- How do you handle disagreement?
- How do you review designs?
