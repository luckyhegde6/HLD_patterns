# Interview Walkthrough

1. Clarify inventory type, peak demand and fairness.
2. Design waiting room and authoritative inventory.
3. Walk hold, payment and confirmation saga.
4. Explain contention, expiry and idempotency.
5. Cover partial failure, ticket issuance and reconciliation.
6. Discuss observability and surge scaling.

Follow-ups: oversell prevention? payment timeout? hold expiry race?
